# Applicant Profiles (Fictional)

Five sample applicants to run through your system. All are invented for the exercise. They are deliberately varied — a good system should not recommend the same plan to all of them, and the reasoning should be specific to each person.

Machine-readable versions are in `hackathon_data.json`.

---

### P1 — Budget-driven young professional
- **Age:** 26 · single · non-smoker
- **Health:** No conditions, no medications
- **Priorities:** Lowest possible premium; basic cover is fine
- **Budget:** Low
- **Near-term needs:** None

### P2 — Planning a family this year
- **Age:** 31 · married · non-smoker
- **Health:** Healthy; **actively planning to conceive within the next 12 months**
- **Priorities:** Maternity coverage that will actually be usable this year; sensible cost
- **Budget:** Moderate
- **Near-term needs:** Maternity, within the year

### P3 — Older applicant with managed chronic conditions
- **Age:** 58 · married · non-smoker
- **Health:** Type 2 diabetes (managed) and hypertension; both stable on medication
- **Priorities:** Good ongoing coverage for existing conditions, but cost genuinely matters
- **Budget:** Moderate
- **Near-term needs:** Continuous management of existing conditions

### P4 — Healthy mid-career, values access
- **Age:** 40 · married · non-smoker
- **Health:** No conditions
- **Priorities:** Solid hospital/network access and reasonable outpatient terms; not chasing the cheapest
- **Budget:** Comfortable
- **Near-term needs:** None specific

### P5 — Older applicant, high needs
- **Age:** 63 · married · non-smoker
- **Health:** Coronary artery disease (prior stent) and type 2 diabetes; higher expected usage
- **Priorities:** Comprehensive coverage and wide hospital access
- **Budget:** Not the primary concern
- **Near-term needs:** Ongoing specialist and cardiac care
